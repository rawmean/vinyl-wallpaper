What Vinyl Wallpaper Does

Your music deserves more than a menu bar widget.

🎵

Desktop Artwork

Full-screen animated wallpaper showing the current album art on a beautiful turntable with a spinning record, tonearm, and VU meters — across all your displays.

💻

Notch Player

A sleek, collapsible player that lives in your MacBook's notch. See the current track, artwork, and controls at a glance without leaving your workflow.

🎧

Multi-Source

Works with Spotify, WiiM streamers, Apple Music, and AirPlay. Switch between sources with one click. Vinyl Wallpaper auto-detects what's playing.

🎛️

Multiple Visual Setups

Choose from album canvas, classic turntable deck, floating vinyl, listening room, Side A label, and After Dark — each with its own personality.

⏱️

Live Progress & Controls

Track progress bar, play/pause, skip, and volume control — all accessible from the wallpaper overlay and the notch player.

🔐

Private by Design

Everything runs locally on your Mac. No data is ever sent to external servers. Your listening habits stay yours.
